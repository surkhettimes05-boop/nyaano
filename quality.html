The NYAANO standard

Trust is an operating system,
not a sticker.

We want every promise on the jar to correspond to something real behind the jar.

01

Source identity

Cow and buffalo are separate product identities. Supplier specifications, manufacturing records and labels should preserve that distinction.

02

Batch accountability

Each production run should be assigned a batch code that can connect the finished pack to production and QC records.

03

Release standard

Sensory, packaging and appropriate analytical requirements should be defined before production—not invented after complaints appear.

04

Pack integrity

Tamper-evident closure, readable dates, net quantity and storage information form part of product quality.

05

Accountability after sale

A real brand should be reachable when something is wrong, with complaint handling and batch traceability built into the system.

Designed for proof

Simple on the front.
Serious underneath.

The front of pack should remain calm. Detailed traceability, batch and quality information belongs where customers and retailers can find it without overwhelming the brand.

Claim policy

We would rather say less and prove it.

NYAANO should not use “A2,” “organic,” “grass-fed,” “bilona,” medical benefits or superiority claims unless they are genuinely supported by the specific supply chain, process and evidence.
