From source to shelf

The whole process,
made visible.

Simple on the shelf. Serious underneath. This is the operating flow NYAANO is being built around.

01

Partner selection

Approve capable manufacturing and supply partners against quality, segregation, documentation and commercial criteria.

02

Source identity

Cow Ghee and Buffalo Ghee remain separate product identities with separate specifications and records.

03

Production

Manufacture to the agreed sensory and technical specification rather than accepting a generic white-label product.

04

Quality release

Check the batch against defined product and packaging requirements before release.

05

Fill, code & seal

Apply clear labels, batch identity and tamper-evident packaging.

06

Warehouse & distribution

Move finished goods through retail, wholesale and future foodservice channels with controlled replenishment.

07

Feedback loop

Use retailer feedback, complaints and repeat-purchase signals to improve the next batch and the operating system.
