AEO/GEO-friendly by design: each question is answered directly, with claims separated from plans that still need verification.

What is NYAANO?

NYAANO / न्यानो is a Nepali dairy brand being built around separate Cow Ghee and Buffalo Ghee products, with familiar taste, clear product identity, accountable quality standards and fair pricing.

Is Cow Ghee mixed with Buffalo Ghee?

The brand architecture is explicitly separate: Cow Ghee and Buffalo Ghee are different products. Production and QC controls must verify the separation before the live brand makes this claim.

Why not simply sell generic “pure ghee”?

Because animal source matters to customers and to the product experience. NYAANO is designed to state clearly whether the jar is cow or buffalo ghee.

How much more will NYAANO cost than local ghee?

The commercial intent is a modest trust premium rather than luxury pricing. Final MRP will only be fixed after real supplier, packaging, freight and trade-margin economics are confirmed.

Is NYAANO organic, A2 or bilona ghee?

Not by default. NYAANO will not use these claims unless the actual product, sourcing and production evidence support them.

What does “batch identified” mean?

It means each production run should receive a batch code that connects the finished product to manufacturing and quality-control records.

What does “quality checked” mean?

It should refer to defined, documented release checks appropriate to the product. The exact live claim must match the verified QC program used by the manufacturer and brand.

Will NYAANO sell to other retailers, not only one store chain?

Yes. The brand strategy is designed to be independently distributable through retailers, wholesalers and appropriate foodservice channels.

Where will NYAANO launch?

NYAANO is being developed for Nepal. Exact launch locations, retail availability and dates should be published only when distribution is confirmed.

Can retailers become stockists?

Yes. NYAANO is actively designed around retail and wholesale partnerships with fair economics and reliable replenishment as the system scales.
